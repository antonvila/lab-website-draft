About

Associate Professor of Genetics, Universidade da Coruña (UDC), Spain.

Antón Vila‑Sanjurjo leads a research program focused on ribosome structure and function, mitochondrial translation, and translational fidelity, with particular emphasis on conceptual and structure‑based frameworks to interpret mitochondrial rRNA variation in human disease.

He received his Ph.D. in Biology from Brown University (Providence, Rhode Island, USA) in May 2001. His doctoral research, supervised by Albert E. Dahlberg, examined the genetic and structural basis of decoding accuracy in the Escherichia coli 16S rRNA decoding center (thesis title: “Genetic Analysis of the Decoding Center of E. coli 16S rRNA”).

He has held research appointments at the Howard Hughes Medical Institute (HHMI) and Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory (LBNL), where he co‑directed the Synthetic Biology project “Reversing the Endosymbiosis of Mitochondria (REM)”, and later served as Senior Scientist at University College Cork.

Prior to formal academic training, he acquired professional experience outside academia in business and accounting.
