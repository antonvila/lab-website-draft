About

Antón Vila‑Sanjurjo is Associate Professor of Genetics at the Universidade da Coruña (UDC), Spain.

He received his Ph.D. in Biology from Brown University (Providence, Rhode Island, USA) in May 2001. His doctoral research, supervised by Albert E. Dahlberg, examined the genetic and structural basis of decoding accuracy in the Escherichia coli 16S rRNA decoding center.

This site presents research from the AVS Lab, focused on ribosome structure and function, mitochondrial translation, and translational fidelity. Current work centres on the mitochondrial ribosome and on the development of Heterologous Inferential Analysis (HIA), a structure‑based framework to interpret mitochondrial rRNA variation in human disease. More broadly, the lab maintains a wide and integrative interest in biology, approaching diverse systems — from bacterial signalling to host–pathogen interactions and fungal evolution — as complementary avenues to uncover general biological principles.
