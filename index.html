Antón Vila‑Sanjurjo

This site presents research from my laboratory, focused on ribosome structure, mitochondrial translation, and translational fidelity.

Current work centers on the mitochondrial ribosome and on the development of Heterologous Inferential Analysis (HIA) to interpret mitochondrial rRNA variants.
