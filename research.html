Research

Research in the lab focuses on the molecular mechanisms that ensure accuracy during protein synthesis, with particular emphasis on ribosome structure and function, mitochondrial translation, and translational fidelity.

A central line of work in the lab is the study of the mitochondrial ribosome and its role in human disease. Mutations affecting mitochondrial ribosomal RNA (mt‑rRNA) have proven particularly challenging to interpret, due to the lack of tractable experimental systems to assess their functional impact directly.

To address this limitation, we developed Heterologous Inferential Analysis (HIA), a framework that leverages the evolutionary conservation of ribosomal structure and decoding mechanisms to infer the functional consequences of mitochondrial rRNA variants using information from bacterial ribosome genetics and high‑resolution structural models.
