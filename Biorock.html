Biorock Technology

Financiado por el Fondo Europeo Marítimo, de Pesca y de Acuicultura (FEMPA) y el Ministerio del Mar

COLLARATION AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE GOVERNMENT OF GALICIA AND THE UNIVERSITY OF A CORUÑA FOR THE STUDY OF IMPROVING THE GROWTH OF AQUACULTURE SPECIES (BIOROCK)"

Funded by the European Maritime, Fisheries and Aquaculture Fund (FEMPA) and the Ministry of the Sea

In collaboration with Global Coral Reef Alliance (GCRA), we have used Biorock technology, based on the application of direct current electricity at very low voltage, to a controlled experimental system, capable of keeping mussels alive for long periods of time. Previous experiments carried out by our collaborators had already shown that this technology has the potential to improve the settlement, growth, and survival of many different types of marine organisms. In addition, Biorock technology has been successfully used in the regeneration of natural marine habitats. The observed proliferation of a wide range of key marine species, including the mussel, as well as the ability to induce the regeneration of the marine habitat, prompted this initiative, specifically, the investigation under controlled experimental conditions, of the potential of Biorock technology for the improvement of the aquaculture of mussels (Mytilus galloprovincialis) in Galicia.
