Collaborations Our research relies on long‑standing national and international collaborations spanning ribosome structure, mitochondrial biology, translational fidelity, and systems biology. We also maintain strong interests in other fields such as bacterial mechanisms of stress response and to the application of molecular biology biochemistry and systems biology to the study of pathogen-host interactions in aquaculture and to the understading of fungal evolution.
