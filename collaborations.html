Collaborations

Our research relies on long‑standing national and international collaborations spanning ribosome structure, mitochondrial biology, translational fidelity, and systems biology. We also maintain strong interests in additional areas, including bacterial mechanisms of stress responses, as well as in the application of molecular biology, biochemistry, and systems biology approaches to the study of pathogen–host interactions in aquaculture and to advancing our understanding of fungal evolution.
