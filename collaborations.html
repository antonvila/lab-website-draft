Collaborations

Our research relies on long‑standing international collaborations spanning ribosome structure, mitochondrial biology, translational fidelity, and systems biology.

Mitochondrial biology and human disease

  • Wellcome Centre for Mitochondrial Research, Newcastle University (UK)
    Mitochondrial translation, mt‑rRNA variants, human disease
  • Institute of Mitochondrial Biology, University of Cologne (Germany)
    Mitochondrial genetics and cancer metabolism

Ribosome structure and translational fidelity

  • Atkins Laboratory, University College Cork (Ireland)
    Translational fidelity and recoding mechanisms
  • Dahlberg Laboratory, Brown University (USA)
    Ribosome genetics and decoding accuracy
  • Cate Laboratory, University of California, Berkeley (USA)
    Structural biology of bacterial ribosomes

Systems biology and immunology

  • Ballard Laboratory, University of New South Wales (Australia)
    Mitonuclear interactions and genotype‑by‑environment effects
  • Lanata / Creech / Pollett Consortium
    Systems biology of immune responses and vaccination

Former and long‑term institutional collaborations

  • Howard Hughes Medical Institute (HHMI) – USA
    Synthetic biology and mitochondrial evolution (REM project)
  • Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory (LBNL) – USA
    Mitochondrial systems biology and modeling
